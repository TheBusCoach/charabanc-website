Questions

School Bus Financing FAQ

Do you finance school buses?
Yes. We finance new and used school buses for public school districts, private schools, daycares, and student transportation contractors. Districts can use a municipal lease. See our used bus financing page for pre-owned units.
What kinds of operators use school bus financing?
Public school districts, private and parochial schools, daycare and Head Start programs, special-needs and paratransit student transportation, and contractors running student routes under contract to a school or district.
What credit do I need to finance a school bus?
We work across a wide credit window, from A+ to C, and can underwrite start-up operators who have good personal credit.
Is 100% financing available for a school bus?
Yes, 100% financing is available for qualified borrowers, along with 90-day deferred payment options.
Loan or TRAC lease: which is better for a school bus?
It depends on your tax position and how long you plan to run the vehicle. A loan builds equity from day one; a TRAC lease sets a residual value up front, which usually means a lower monthly payment. See the side-by-side comparison on our motorcoach financing page, and confirm tax treatment with your accountant.
What do I need to apply?
Smaller requests can go application-only: a credit application, recent bank statements, and a short company overview. Larger requests use our full-financials application. Both are on our Resources page.
